What's included

Two services. Take one or both.

01 — Interior

Interior detailing

  • Full vacuum — seats, carpets, trunk, under the seats
  • Shampoo and hot-water extraction on cloth seats and mats
  • Leather cleaned and conditioned
  • Dash, console, vents, cup holders and door panels degreased
  • Pet hair and stain removal

  • Glass, mirrors and door jambs finished
  • Engine bay dusted down and dressed
